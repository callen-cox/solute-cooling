The idea

Dissolving some salts in water absorbs heat, the chemistry behind an instant cold pack. Instead of a radiator, this system keeps a tank of coolant and doses solute in only when the servers need it. That suits places with no room or no outside access for a radiator.

Safety ladder (live)

White marker = current temperature. Warn: alert + maximum dosing. Cut: IT load halved. Off: safe shutdown, latched until cooled. Hardware damage is far above, with a guaranteed margin.

Four steps, every second

  1. Sense: three sensors are median-voted. A lying sensor is outvoted; no usable data means fail-safe shutdown.
  2. Predict: the temperature trend is projected 60 s ahead, so cooling starts before it gets hot.
  3. Dose: a PI controller sets grams of solute per second and learns if the pump under-delivers.
  4. Protect: NORMAL → WARNING → THROTTLE → SHUTDOWN, with hysteresis so it can't flap on and off.
