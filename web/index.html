The idea

Small data centers often have no room for a radiator: an office building's server closet, a cramped site in a developing region, or a relay in orbit. This controller treats the coolant as a finite thermal sink and manages it autonomously, with a safety supervisor that can always overrule it.

Three layers, every control step

1 · Safety supervisor (highest priority) overrides everything: NORMAL → WARNING → THROTTLE → SHUTDOWN.
2 · Optimisation mode Efficiency, Performance or Stealth decides how to spend the cooling.
3 · Cooling sink Salt dissolution, PCM modules or orbital radiators do the physical work.

Safety ladder (live)

White marker = current temperature. Warn (52 °C): alert and maximum cooling. Cut (56 °C): IT load halved, held at least 5 min. Off (60 °C, Tsafe): clean shutdown, latched until the coolant is back under 46 °C. Hardware damage is at 85 °C, and the config refuses any setting with under 10 °C of margin. In orbit a freeze guard also protects the bottom of the range.

Every step

  1. Sense: three sensors are median-voted. One liar is outvoted. With no usable data it assumes the worst and shuts down.
  2. Predict: the temperature trend is projected 60 s ahead (10 min in orbit), and server power is fed forward, so cooling starts before the temperature moves.
  3. Supervise: the ladder above. Running low on cooling escalates early. Running out triggers a graceful power-down.
  4. Act: the mode drives the sink (below).

The three cooling sinks

SinkPhysicsWhat the controller does
Salt
terrestrial		Dissolving NH4Cl absorbs 277 J/g (ΔHsoln = +14.8 kJ/mol), like an instant cold pack. It is consumed: about 13 kg per kWh. Doses grams per second (feedforward + predictive PI), learns if the pump under-delivers, and tracks saturation against the coldest planned temperature, because NH4Cl is less soluble when cold. At 85% it swaps to a reserve tank. With no reserve it stops dosing before crystals clog the cold plates.
PCM
terrestrial		Sodium thiosulfate pentahydrate melts at 48.3 °C and absorbs about 200 kJ/kg doing it, then re-solidifies to be reused. Runs one module at a time and swaps when it is spent. When the modules are half spent it schedules a low-power state so idle ones re-solidify.
Radiators
orbital		No air in space, so heat only leaves as radiation: Q = εσAT⁴. Sunlight adds αSA·cos θ. Keeps the radiators edge-on to the sun. If they absorb more than they emit (stuck facing the sun), it retracts them behind the MLI shield. A freeze guard (heater, sun-facing, retraction) keeps the water above 5 °C.
